## Article status

The paper is an author-prepared preprint submitted to *Materials for Quantum
Technology*, before peer review and publisher editing. It is not the journal
Version of Record. An arXiv link and the journal citation/DOI will be added when
available.

The tutorial introduces ISE for van der Waals materials through a worked MoS₂
example, covering measurement settings, contrast screening, ROI selection,
optical modelling, uncertainty and comparison with complementary measurements.

This initial repository shares the paper, video and accompanying presentation
material. The supporting experimental dataset is being prepared separately and
is not included here. Further companion resources will be linked when available.

Questions and collaboration enquiries: lmh207@cam.ac.uk.
